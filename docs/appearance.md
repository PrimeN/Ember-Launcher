# Make Ember look like your TV

Ember's appearance is a set of independent choices. A Home layout decides where
apps and media live; a colour palette changes the interface colours. Changing one
does not require changing the others.

## Pick a Home interface

Choose **Settings → Appearance → Interface**.

| Interface | What changes | Suits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ember** | Large featured artwork, configurable media rows, Home widgets and a separate navbar | Browsing films, shows, libraries and channels from Home |
| **Ember Light** | A six-across app layout, glass app dock, carousel and compact status controls | Opening apps first, with media nearby |

**Ember Light is a layout, not a light colour theme.** Both interfaces have their
own day/night appearance. Light has its own navigation: page shortcuts behave
like app tiles and support moving and hiding. Its navbar cannot be replaced with
one of Ember's separate navbar styles.

Ember Light offers **Title art**, **Aurora**, **Ocean**, **Ember glow** and **Black**
backgrounds. Its carousel can use configured content and participating apps'
Continue watching or recommendations. Those app rows require the appropriate
device access and content supplied by the other apps. Idle expansion and motion
are configurable.

## Choose navigation

On the Ember interface, open **Appearance → Navbar style**.

| Style | Appearance |
| --- | --- |
| **Ember** | The default rounded navigation pill, separate profile and quick-action controls |
| **Ember Collapsed** | A compact current-page handle that expands to show navigation |
| **Cinema Bar** | A slim, horizontal cinema-style header |

The default Ember navbar can use a top or side position. Visible tabs are
configurable. Tabs for optional features depend on their configuration; a new
installation need not show every destination.

## Choose a media-page layout

**Appearance → Media page layout** is independent of the Home interface.

| Layout | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Ember** | A broad artwork-led details page with playback actions and media sections below |
| **Ember X** | Cinematic artwork with episode and source selection in a right-hand panel |

Ember X here names a **media-page layout**, not another Home interface or a colour
palette. On a small viewport or with enlarged text, the layout can adapt to keep
its content usable. Available source badges and playback choices depend on the
configured add-ons, servers and media.

## Pick player controls separately

Open **Settings → Player → Player interface**. The current choices are:

| Controls | Presentation |
| --- | --- |
| **Ember** | Compact playback controls |
| **Glass** | A floating glass-style transport dock |
| **Basic** | A restrained cinema-style control layout |
| **Streaming** | Transport controls and title across the top, with track controls near the timeline |

This choice changes the controls, not the playback engine. Standard and
Compatibility are engine choices; device decoding and output capabilities still
determine what can play.

## Choose colours and brightness

Open **Appearance → Colour theme**.

| Palette | Character |
| --- | --- |
| **Ember** | Warm orange accents and charcoal surfaces |
| **Charcoal** | Neutral dark grey surfaces and cool grey accents |
| **Green** | Green focus and gently tinted surfaces |
| **Rose** | Rose accents and softly tinted surfaces |
| **Android TV** | Cool slate/navy surfaces, pale focus and blue selected-state accents |
| **Red** | Dark charcoal surfaces with cinema-red accents |

Palettes style the interface. They do not turn your artwork monochrome. Choose
**Day**, **Night** or **Auto** separately, with an optional OLED-black preference.
The same Home layout can therefore look quite different without changing how it
navigates.

## Tune density and effects

- **Size & spacing:** interface size, text size, media-card density and screen margins.
- **Apps & folders:** icon shape, labels, app artwork, folders, ordering and hidden apps.
- **Performance & effects:** artwork quality, glass, focus feedback, animations,
  colour analysis and prefetch controls.
- **Widgets & collections:** choose rows, numbered rows, cinematic carousels or
  hero banners, their source content and where they appear on Home.

**Auto** selects a rendering budget for the device. **Low** prioritizes constrained
devices, **Medium** retains restrained motion, and **High** permits richer effects.
**Ultra (4K)** is offered on supported 4K displays. **Custom** separates the controls
so, for example, high-quality artwork can be combined with reduced effects.
These are launcher-rendering choices, not a switch that reduces video quality.

For a simple starting point, use Ember + the Ember navbar + the Ember media page +
Glass playback controls, then choose a palette. For an app-first TV, select Ember
Light and customize its app dock instead.
